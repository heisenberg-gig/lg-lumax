Settings → Picture follows the TV's own menu: OLED pixel brightness, contrast, black level (the 0–100 slider), dynamic contrast, peak brightness, gamma (2.2 or BT.1886), sharpness, super resolution and noise reduction, with Off/Low/Medium/High translated. The Signal card shows the video format and the audio format with its Dolby Atmos layer. Also fixes the session timer and remote battery readings.
